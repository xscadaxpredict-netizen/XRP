How Automated Ratings are Calculated

📦 Quality Rating

Based on items **Accepted** vs. **Rejected** in GRNs.

Score = (Accepted Items / Received Items) × 5.0

Example: 10 items received, 8 accepted = 4.0 ⭐

🚚 Delivery Rating

Based on **Expected Delivery Date** vs. **Actual GRN Date**.

Score = Max(5.0 - (Days Delayed × 0.5), 1.0)

Rule: Perfect on-time delivery is 5.0 ⭐. Every day late reduces the score by 0.5 stars.

💰 Pricing Rating

Compares vendor's unit price with the **System Average** for that same product.

Score = (Average Peer Price / Vendor Price) × 5.0

Rule: If price is average or below, 5.0 ⭐. If more expensive than peers, score drops proportionally.
